# Say once

Use only when named. Same rule for new prose and for a draft you were given.

The listener is an adult with no special training. They have already heard what this task gave them. If another skill states how much they understand, follow that skill.

Do not use a word that would make them ask "what does that mean?" Do not add a gloss to rescue it. Say that fact in words they already use.

Do not write a sentence that would make them think "you already said that." Do not paraphrase the same meaning, and do not rename it.

Keep what they do not know yet, even when it is long. Do not cut a new point here just because it belongs to another topic.

Return the prose. Add a short bullet list of the "already said" items you left out. When writing new, leave them out from the start and add the same list.
